# Privacy Notice

**EconomicBridge**, operated by **Bizra Farms Integrated Nigeria Limited** (RC 1929412), Kebbi State, Nigeria
Last updated: 25 September 2026

This notice explains:
- what personal data EconomicBridge collects, and why;
- what messages we send, and who we send them to;
- who we share personal data with, and how long we keep it;
- the rights you have under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023**.

## 1. Who is responsible

Bizra Farms Integrated Nigeria Limited is the data controller for personal data processed through economicbridge.org and the EconomicBridge platform.

Our privacy contact is our Data Protection lead: **bizra@economicbridge.org**, +234 703 791 9465.

## 2. What EconomicBridge is, and what it is not

EconomicBridge analyses open satellite and public data, such as vegetation, rainfall, storms, fires, night light and land change. It reports results for areas: states, local government areas, wards and named villages.

- **Population figures are published estimates.** Figures such as "about 9,000 people in this village" come from open datasets. They describe places, not people.
- **We do not identify households or individuals from satellite data.**

## 4. Messages we send

We only send a message where that service is running for your area or organisation. Some of the services below are running today. Others will start as the platform grows, and this notice already covers them.

**You can stop messages at any time** by telling the partner agency that enrolled you, or by contacting us.

**Alerts are not an emergency service.** In an emergency, follow the guidance of NEMA, your State Emergency Management Agency and NiMet.

### To farmers, cooperative leaders and farmer leaders — by SMS, in your language where available

**Service messages:**
- confirmation that you were enrolled;
- how to stop messages;
- changes to the service.

**Weather:**
- **Rainfall advisories** when extreme rain is expected or recorded in your area.
- **Storm notices** after a severe storm is recorded in your local government area.
- **Dry-spell and planting-window advisories** about when the rains start, pause or end.

**Crops:**
- **Crop-stress warnings** when satellites show unusual stress on vegetation in your area, so fields can be scouted for pests, disease or lack of water.
- **Pest and disease notices** when a problem is confirmed in your area.

**Fire:**
- **Fire warnings** in the dry season when active fires are detected near farmland in your area.

**Markets:**
- **Market price updates** for staple crops at nearby markets.

### To field officers and extension agents — by SMS and email

- **Results** of your farm checks and leaf diagnoses.
- **Field tasks:** a place to verify on the ground, with the nearest village and directions. Examples are land change, storm damage or crop stress.
- **Alerts on plots you watch.**
- **Village lists** for enumeration or outreach in your area.

### To organisations and agencies — by email

- **Alert digests by module:** land change on farmland; storms and extreme rainfall; crop health; fires; dry spells.
- **Scheduled reports** for the modules your organisation chose.
- **Notices when new data is published,** such as a new round of village night-light measurements or refreshed aid activity.

### To account users — by email

- Invitations and account activation.
- Password resets.
- **Security notices,** such as a sign-in from a new device, or sign-in codes once two-factor sign-in starts.
- **Service notices,** such as planned maintenance, incidents, and changes to this notice or our terms.

**New channels.** Messages may in future also be sent by voice call or messaging app (for example WhatsApp), to reach people who do not read SMS easily. Before we start a new channel or a new provider for it, we will update this notice.

## 6. International transfers

Our database, file storage and email provider are in Ireland, in the European Union. Some other providers operate outside Nigeria.

We transfer personal data outside Nigeria only as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 permits, to providers bound by contract to protect it with appropriate security.

## 7. How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account data | While the account is active, and 12 months after it is closed |
| Message recipients (phone number and preferences) | Until you or the partner agency ask us to stop |
| Records of messages sent | 3 years |
| Farm checks and leaf photographs | For the term of the organisation's agreement, and 12 months after |
| Records of actions in the platform (audit records) | 3 years |
| Contact enquiries | 24 months, unless they lead to an agreement |
| Data-protection requests | 3 years after they are completed |
| Server logs | 14 days |

**Deleted records and erasure.** The platform keeps an archive of changed and deleted records, so that nothing is lost by mistake. The one exception is erasure: when you ask us to erase your personal data and the law allows it, we delete it and every archived copy of it. We keep only a record that the erasure was carried out, which contains no personal data.

## 8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:
- ask for a copy of your personal data;
- ask us to correct it;
- ask us to erase it;
- ask us to restrict how we use it;
- object to how we use it;
- ask for it in a portable form;
- withdraw consent where we rely on consent, including to stop messages.

To make a request, email **bizra@economicbridge.org**. We will answer within **30 days**.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission**.

## 9. Security

We protect personal data with:
- **Encryption:** in transit (TLS 1.2 and 1.3) and at rest (encrypted database storage).
- **Access controls:** only authorised operators can see accounts, message recipients, subscriptions and data-protection requests.
- **Audit records** of actions taken in the platform.
- **Monitoring and daily backups.**

No system is perfectly secure. We will tell you and the Commission about a breach where the law requires it.

## 10. Children

EconomicBridge is a service for institutions. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal data. Estimates of children under five are area-level figures from published datasets. They are not data about any child.

## 11. Changes to this notice

We will post any update here with a new date. Where a change is significant, we will tell account users by email and message recipients by SMS.
