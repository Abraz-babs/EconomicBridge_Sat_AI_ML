What we don't claim

FloodsOur radar backtest missed the 2024 Kebbi floods, so we report storms and extreme rainfall — not floods.
IncomeNight light shows where a state should look first. It is not a measure of what a household earns.
IndividualsWe report places — villages, wards, fields. We never identify a household or a person from space.
What cloud hidesBenue's 2025 rains were too cloudy for a change baseline. We show that gap instead of filling it.
